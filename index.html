01 / Sense

Start with the conditions.

Light alone doesn’t tell the whole story. Our team’s prototype also reads temperature and humidity, using dew point to help identify fog conditions.

  • Phototransistor
  • Temperature / humidity sensor
02 / Respond

Make the inputs useful.

An Arduino control circuit connects those inputs to the light’s brightness and RGB output. I wired and programmed the circuit and led research and validation testing.

In simulated fog and low light, the prototype’s output shifted correctly. This was a team prototype, not a road-certified product.

03 / Build

Give the idea a physical form.

I modeled the housing and mounts in Onshape, then 3D printed the parts in PLA for assembly and fit testing. The enclosure brought the control circuit and light into one build.
