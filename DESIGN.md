# Design direction

Audience: engineering recruiters, collaborators, and the course instructor. The first screen must establish Abdallah's discipline, show how he thinks, and lead to real projects.

The UI/UX Pro Max search for `engineering portfolio minimal` recommended a spacious Swiss layout with monochrome contrast and restrained interactions. Keep those principles; replace its stock blue accent and software feature pattern with a burgundy engineering annotation color and a project-driven narrative. Frontend-design guidance informed the asymmetric composition and the choice to spend visual emphasis on one exploded headlight assembly rather than repeated decorated cards.

Updated tokens: pale mint #edf4f2, white #ffffff, navy ink #203a43, slate #50686f, burgundy #8a3656, teal #206d72, and ochre #8b5f25. Dark mode uses deep navy #14252d, light text #eff5f3, pale rose #f1aec5, and teal #8ad0ce. Archivo headings, system sans-serif body. Body line length at most 70 characters; 16–18px body; 44px minimum controls.

Home: compact header, large left-aligned introduction beside an exploded headlight, selected work, then a six-chapter sticky story, a short background, and contact. About: education, actual experience, interests. Projects: four detailed case studies with explicit roles, methods, and limitations. Skills: grouped evidence tied to actual projects. Contact: email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and a validated email-draft form.

Motion now follows the owner's request for a richer presentation: cached scroll geometry drives the assembly continuously in either direction. An intersection observer wakes the renderer shortly before the story approaches the viewport; it does not select chapters. This avoids forcing offscreen layout during the initial page load. The six chapters cover sensing, response, CAD design, wiring, enclosure refinement, and the final build. Continuous opacity blends select the actual CAD and photos as each chapter enters the reading area; reversing scroll restores earlier evidence. HTML legends below the diagrams replace overlapping SVG text. A compact sticky visual remains visible on phones; short landscape screens use document flow. No scroll lock or external animation dependency is used. A pausable two-row skills ribbon and small fine-pointer companion ring add motion; the native cursor remains visible. OS reduced motion and a persistent on-page motion control remove nonessential animation, disable the ribbon/cursor, and return the story to ordinary flow.

Protolight now uses genuine prototype photos and CAD images extracted from the supplied capstone notebook. Its explanatory SVG matches the actual L-shaped printed enclosure, LED channels, and sensor placement. The professional and graduation photos retain their original appearance; equal circular headshot frames are cropped with CSS and preview the alternate portrait on fine-pointer hover, restore the resting photo on exit, and flip through horizontal touch swipes or an explicit click/keyboard control. Pending photo loads cannot override a later pointer exit. A sun/moon icon morph accompanies a circular palette reveal from the theme control. Browsers without View Transitions use a color fade; reduced motion switches immediately. Rapid toggles settle on the latest preference. LinkedIn appears in every header and on the portrait pages. See docs/MEDIA.md for provenance. Other project illustrations remain labeled placeholders. Do not publish the original resume, phone number, ZIP code, or street address.
